Branch: `agent/exceptional-judge-experience`

## Required truthfulness boundary

- `/judge` is a deterministic `Demo environment` and `Simulated workflow` scenario.
- Any AI-looking step is labelled `Fixture AI`; it represents structured proposal output, not a live Gemini call.
- Numbers on `/business-model` are illustrative assumptions, not revenue, customer, or traction claims.
- `/evidence` calls out what is implemented in the repository, what was verified by CI, what is demonstrated with fixture data, and what still requires external production verification.
- The live workspace remains API-backed. Existing demo fallback behavior is only allowed under the repository's explicit demo contract.
